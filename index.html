What Is an Inflatable Castle Rental in Round Rock, TX?

An inflatable castle is an airtight vinyl or nylon structure kept inflated by a continuous electric blower, designed for children to jump, bounce, and play inside at parties and events. Inflatable castles are the primary product in the party rental industry - delivered to your backyard, set up by a professional crew, and picked up after your event.

In Round Rock, TX and across the Austin metro area, inflatable castles are most commonly called moonwalks - a Texas-native regional term that comes from the weightless, floating sensation children feel while jumping inside the inflatable chamber. Nationally the standard term is bounce house. Internationally the term is bouncy castle. All refer to the same party rental equipment category.

Moonwalk Rental FAQ - Round Rock TX

Everything Round Rock and Williamson County families ask before booking their party rental.

What is a moonwalk rental in Round Rock TX?
A moonwalk is the Texas term for an inflatable bounce house or bouncy castle. In Round Rock TX and across the Austin metro, locals commonly call bounce houses "moonwalks" - a regional name that comes from the floating, weightless feeling kids get while jumping. The terms moonwalk, bounce house, inflatable castle, bouncy castle, and jump house all refer to the same party rental equipment.
How much does a bounce house rental cost in Round Rock TX?
Inflatable castle rentals in Round Rock TX range from $150–$350 for a standard moonwalk, $225–$400 for a combo castle with slide, and $200–$450 for a water slide castle. All prices include delivery, setup, and teardown. Request a free quote for current pricing.
What size moonwalk fits my Round Rock backyard?
Most standard Round Rock backyard lots in Forest Creek, Teravista, and Avery Ranch fit a 13x13 or 15x15 moonwalk. Combo units with slides need roughly 20x20 feet. Water slides need more open space - 15ft slides require about 25x25 feet of flat area. We'll recommend the right fit when you book.
Do I need a permit for a bounce house at a Round Rock park?
Yes - Round Rock parks require a permit and reservation for bounce house and moonwalk setups. Contact Round Rock Parks and Recreation at (512) 218-5540. Most parks need a power outlet or generator. Old Settlers Park has electricity at two locations. Rabb Park does not allow inflatables. Always confirm before booking.
What surface do you need to set up a moonwalk or bounce house?
Moonwalks and bounce houses can be set up on grass, packed dirt, pavement, and some indoor surfaces. Grass is preferred for staking. Pavement setups use sandbags. The area must be flat, level, and clear of overhead obstructions. Most units need a standard 110V outlet within 50 feet - or a generator for park setups.
Can bounce houses be used in rain or wind in Texas?
Bounce houses and moonwalks can run in light rain with supervision. They must be shut down in winds over 15–20 mph and never operated during lightning. If severe weather forces cancellation, we work with you to reschedule at no extra charge. Safety always comes first.
How long is the rental period for a moonwalk in Round Rock?
Standard moonwalk and bounce house rentals in Round Rock TX are for the full event day - typically 6 to 8 hours. We deliver before your event starts and return for pickup afterward. Extended periods are available. We arrive early so everything is fully inflated and safety-inspected before your first guest arrives.
How many kids can use a bounce house at once?
A standard 13x13 inflatable castle safely accommodates 4–6 children at a time. Larger combo castle units handle 6–8 riders simultaneously. Always follow the operator's capacity limits and supervise children during use. We provide capacity guidelines with every rental.
Are your moonwalks and inflatables licensed and insured in Texas?
Yes. All rentals are provided by Jumpin Joy Party Rentals, who is fully licensed and insured in Williamson County, Texas. In Texas, bounce houses and moonwalks are classified as Class B Amusement Rides requiring a current Texas Amusement Ride Compliance Sticker and minimum $1,000,000 liability coverage per occurrence. Equipment is sanitized between every rental. See our About Us page for more detail.
What is the difference between a moonwalk, bounce house, and bouncy castle?
They are all the same thing - different regional names for the same party rental equipment. Moonwalk is the Texas term (Round Rock, Austin, San Antonio, Houston). Bounce house is the US national standard. Inflatable castle is the international term. Bouncy castle is the British term. All describe an airtight inflatable where children jump and play.
