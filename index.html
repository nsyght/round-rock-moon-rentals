What Is a Moonwalk Rental in Round Rock, TX?

A moonwalk is the Texas term for an inflatable bounce house. In Round Rock, Pflugerville, Hutto, and across the greater Austin area, locals commonly call bounce houses "moonwalks" — a regional name that comes from the floating, weightless sensation children feel while jumping inside the inflatable chamber. If you're searching for a moonwalk rental in Round Rock TX, you're looking for the same thing as a bounce house rental.

We deliver moonwalk rentals throughout Round Rock (zip codes 78664, 78665, and 78681) and all of Williamson County. Every moonwalk is delivered fully inflated, safety-inspected, and ready to bounce — your crew handles setup and teardown so you can enjoy the party.

Moonwalk = Bounce House

In Texas, "moonwalk" and "bounce house" refer to the same inflatable unit. Both terms describe a sealed inflatable chamber kids jump inside at parties.

Combo Moonwalk

A combo moonwalk adds a built-in water or dry slide to the bounce chamber — two activities in one footprint. The most popular rental in Round Rock.

Water Slide Moonwalk

A standalone inflatable water slide — no bounce chamber. Ranges from 15 to 20+ feet tall. The top summer rental in Round Rock from March through September.

Why "Moonwalk"?

The name comes from the weightless, floating feeling kids experience when jumping. The term is used throughout Texas and parts of the Southern US — especially in the Austin, Houston, and San Antonio markets.
