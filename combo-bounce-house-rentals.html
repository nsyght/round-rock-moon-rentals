What Is a Combo Unit?

Bounce House & Water Slide in One

A combo bounce house rental - also called a combo moonwalk rental in Texas - is a single inflatable unit that packs a full bounce chamber and a built-in slide into one footprint. Instead of renting two separate units, one combo does it all. It's the most popular rental choice for Round Rock backyard birthday parties in Forest Creek, Teravista, and Avery Ranch because it delivers maximum fun per square foot.

Most of our combo units run wet or dry - run the slide dry in the spring, hook up the water line and turn it into a water slide combo all summer. One unit, two seasons of fun.

What is a combo bounce house rental in Round Rock TX?
A combo bounce house rental in Round Rock TX is an inflatable that combines a bounce chamber and a water slide in one footprint - also called a combo moonwalk rental in Texas. Can combo bounce houses be used as water slides?
Yes. Most combo units run wet or dry. In wet mode the built-in slide becomes a water slide with a splash pool landing area. In dry mode the same unit works as a standard bounce and slide combo - perfect for Round Rock TX weather all season long.

How is a combo moonwalk different from a regular moonwalk in Texas?
A standard moonwalk (bounce house) in Texas is a single bounce chamber with no slide. A combo moonwalk adds a built-in water or dry slide attached to the bounce chamber - giving you twice the activity in the same footprint. Combo units typically cost slightly more but are the better value for parties with 10 or more kids.
